## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**
Sci-fi space station survival. The game takes place on Viltrum, a remote research station that has been seized by a hostile intruder. The tone is tense and exploratory, with the player searching the station's rooms for the gear needed to fight back.

**Storyline:**
The player is the last free crew member aboard Viltrum, a deep-space research station that has been taken over by the warlord Kregg, who now controls the station from the Throne Room and plans to use its data and systems for his own conquest. Starting in the Main Hub, the player must explore the station and gather six items: the Data Stick, Navigation Map, Keycard, Bandage, Battery Cell, and GreatSword. Only by collecting all six can the player be ready to face Kregg. If the player enters the Throne Room without every item, Kregg wins and the station is lost.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Main Hub (Start room): The central hub of the station. It connects to the Science Laboratory (north), Crew Quarters (west), Medical Bay (south), and Weapon Room (east). No item.
2. Science Laboratory: North of the Main Hub. Connects to the Observation Tower (east). Item: Data Stick.
3. Observation Tower: East of the Science Laboratory. Item: Navigation Map.
4. Crew Quarters: West of the Main Hub. Item: Keycard.
5. Medical Bay: South of the Main Hub. Connects to the Maintenance Room (east). Item: Bandage.
6. Maintenance Room: East of the Medical Bay. Item: Battery Cell.
7. Weapon Room: East of the Main Hub. Connects to the Throne Room (north). Item: GreatSword.
8. Throne Room (Villain room): North of the Weapon Room. Home of Kregg. No item.

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Data Stick (Science Laboratory): Holds the station's system data and Kregg's access codes.
2. Navigation Map (Observation Tower): Shows the layout of the station and the safest route to the Throne Room.
3. Keycard (Crew Quarters): Grants access to restricted areas of the station.
4. Bandage (Medical Bay): Lets the player recover from injuries during the fight.
5. Battery Cell (Maintenance Room): Powers the equipment needed to confront Kregg.
6. GreatSword (Weapon Room): The weapon the player uses to defeat Kregg.

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

**Kregg** is the warlord who has taken over Viltrum and rules it from the Throne Room. He is powerful and ruthless, and he holds the station's crew and systems hostage. The player cannot beat him unprepared, so they must collect all six items before entering the Throne Room. Entering without them means defeat.
